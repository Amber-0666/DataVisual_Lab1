Appliance Energy Consumption in Australia

Australian households rely on a growing number of electrical appliances every day, from refrigerators and washing machines to entertainment systems and heating/cooling units. Understanding how much energy these appliances consume — and what that means for household power bills and national electricity demand — is an important part of energy literacy.
